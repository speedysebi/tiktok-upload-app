1. What Clip Relay is

Clip Relay is a private desktop tool that one person, the account owner, runs on their own computer. It sends short video clips the owner has edited to the owner's own TikTok accounts, as drafts in the TikTok inbox. The owner then reviews, captions and publishes each draft by hand in the TikTok app. Clip Relay never publishes anything by itself.

Clip Relay is not offered to the public. It has no sign-up and no accounts of its own. It is used only with TikTok accounts that belong to the account owner.

2. Who may use it

Only the account owner may use Clip Relay, and only with TikTok accounts they own and control. Connecting someone else's TikTok account is not allowed.

3. Your responsibilities

When you use Clip Relay, you agree to:

  • follow TikTok's Terms of Service, Community Guidelines and Developer Terms;
  • upload only content you have the right to post, including permission from anyone who appears in it or owns it;
  • review every draft yourself before you publish it.

Clip Relay uses TikTok's official APIs (Login Kit and the Content Posting API). It is not made, endorsed or supported by TikTok. You can disconnect Clip Relay at any time in the TikTok app's settings, under the apps and services that can access your account.
