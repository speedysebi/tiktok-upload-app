Clip Relay

Clip Relay is a private desktop tool. One person, the account owner, runs it on their own computer to send short video clips they have edited to their own TikTok accounts, as drafts in the TikTok inbox.

How it works

  1. The owner connects their own TikTok account once, through TikTok's official login (Login Kit).
  2. Clip Relay uploads an edited clip to that account's TikTok inbox as a draft, using TikTok's Content Posting API.
  3. The owner opens the draft in the TikTok app, reviews it, adds the caption and publishes it by hand. Clip Relay never publishes anything by itself.

Who it is for

Only the account owner, and only with TikTok accounts they own. Clip Relay is not offered to the public: it has no sign-up and no accounts of its own.

Permissions

Clip Relay asks TikTok for two permissions: user.info.basic, to check the connection still works, and video.upload, to send drafts to the inbox. See the Privacy Policy for what is stored and the Terms of Service for the rules of use.

Clip Relay is not made, endorsed or supported by TikTok. Contact: open an issue on GitHub.
